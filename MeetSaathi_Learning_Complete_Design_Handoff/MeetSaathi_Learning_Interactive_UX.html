Here's the idea.

Memoization means remembering the answer to a subproblem so you don't have to calculate it again.

Simple example

If the same calculation occurs five times, solve it once, store the result, and reuse it.

What's confusing?
Why is memoization different from ordinary recursion?
Recursion describes how a function solves smaller instances. Memoization is an added optimization that stores results of repeated instances.

Product decisions

Start with clarity

One prominent call to action, last course in context, and immediate access to study. No wall of unrelated features.

Why this design
  • Begins from learning intent, not AI model choice.
  • Supports session capture and post-class revision.
  • No compulsory account integrations.
Build standard

Interaction reference only. The production product requires real mobile audio routing, authorized capture, grounded AI processing, session recovery, accessibility and comprehensive quality tests.
